What's Your Attachment Style?

Before you start the quiz, let's understand together what attachment theory is and why it makes such a big difference in how you understand your relationships.

What Is Attachment Theory?

Attachment theory is a psychological theory that explains how our earliest experiences — especially in childhood with the most important people in our lives — shape the way we handle closeness, trust, and the fear of loss as we grow up. Your attachment style isn't a fixed fate; it's a lens that helps you understand why you respond in a certain way in certain situations.

Secure

Comfortable with both closeness and independence, trusts relatively easily, and communicates clearly when something feels off.

Anxious

Needs constant reassurance, is very sensitive to any sign of distance, and fears being abandoned or losing the other person's interest.

Avoidant

Prefers independence, finds deep emotional closeness difficult, and tends to withdraw when feelings or commitment grow bigger.

Fearful-Avoidant (Disorganized)

A mix of a genuine desire for closeness and a fear of it at the same time — an inner contradiction that can be hard to understand.

Understanding your style isn't about putting yourself in a fixed box — it's about understanding your own reactions and your partner's more deeply, and judging both less. Ready to discover your style?
