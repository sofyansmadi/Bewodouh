Before You Book

Frequently Asked Questions

Are consultations held remotely or in person?
All consultations are held remotely (voice or video call) via a secure link sent to you after your booking is confirmed, fitting your schedule and protecting your privacy.
Who are the specialists I'll be talking to?
Licensed mental health professionals whose qualifications we verify before they join the Bewoduh team. After booking, we assign you the most suitable specialist based on the type of consultation you chose.
What if the first specialist isn't the right fit for me?
You can request to change specialists at any time without any hassle — our goal is for you to feel comfortable enough for the consultation to actually be helpful.
Is my data and my conversation with the specialist confidential?
Yes. All your sessions are confidential between you and the specialist, and your personal data is protected and never shared with any third party. See our Privacy Policy for more details.
Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?
Yes, you can reschedule your appointment up to 24 hours before the session at no extra charge.

Important note: Consultations provided by the Bewoduh team complement medical care and are not a substitute for it in cases requiring specialized medical diagnosis or treatment. If your physical or psychological safety is directly at risk, seek immediate help from a specialized support service or the nearest emergency service.
