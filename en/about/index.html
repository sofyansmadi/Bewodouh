About Us

We started with a simple question: why is it so hard to notice we're in a toxic relationship while we're inside it?

Bewoduh isn't just another blog — it's an attempt to build a trustworthy, honest reference on relationships and personality patterns, in language that feels close to people, not an academic lecture that feels distant from them.

The Story

Where the Idea Started

There's a lot of content out there about relationships, but most of it is either exaggerated — turning every disagreement into a "toxic relationship" — or academic and far from the language people actually use to talk about their lives. Bewoduh came to fill that middle ground: honest, direct content built on reliable sources, but written in the language of someone talking with you, not lecturing you.

The goal isn't to tell you who's right and who's wrong, but to help you see the full picture so you can decide for yourself.

We started with "recognizing a toxic relationship" because it's the question we get most, the one on so many people's minds: "What's happening to me, and why can't I put my finger on it?" From here, we're gradually growing to cover a complete journey — from awareness, to healing, to building healthy relationships from the ground up.

Our Methodology

How We Build Every Piece of Content

01

Built on Established Frameworks

We draw on well-established concepts in psychology (attachment theory, known patterns of psychological manipulation) instead of inventing our own labels.

02

Describing Behavior, Not Diagnosing

We focus on observable patterns and behaviors, not on final medical labels for anyone.

03

Real Language

Realistic examples and dialogue, in clear, easy-to-understand language, far from academic complexity.

In Complete Honesty

We are not licensed mental health therapists. Our content is built on deep reading and continuous engagement with reliable sources in psychology, and on a human understanding of real experiences — not on an academic degree in psychology.

For us, this isn't a weakness we hide — it's a commitment we state clearly: all content aims to raise awareness and encourage reflection, and is not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment with a licensed professional. If you feel you need professional support, we always encourage you to seek it.
