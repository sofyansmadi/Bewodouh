About Us

We help you understand yourself clearly

Bewoduh is an Arabic-language platform for counselling, psychotherapy and personal development.

Our Idea

Simple and clear

Many people go through hard times without knowing where to start. Bewoduh is a place where someone listens and walks with you, in your language and with your privacy respected.

We believe understanding yourself is the start of every change.

We bring specialists, counsellors and coaches together in one place, with sessions, articles and quizzes that guide you step by step.

Our Team

Three roles, one goal

01

Psychologists & therapists

They provide psychotherapy and counselling for situations that need professional follow-up.

02

Counsellors

They offer psychological counselling and support, and walk with you through difficult times.

03

Coaches

They help you develop yourself, set your goals and reach them.

In Honesty

We review the qualifications and certificates of everyone who joins our team before their profile is published.

Coaching and self-development do not replace therapy when it is needed, and our articles and quizzes are for awareness, not diagnosis. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.
