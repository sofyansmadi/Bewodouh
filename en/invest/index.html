About us

Bewoduh: therapy sessions in Arabic, with specialists whose credentials we review

A platform that connects people looking for psychological support or guidance with psychology specialists and life skills coaches, through online sessions in Arabic, for the Arab world and Arabs abroad. It is operated by LAMSA LTD, registered in England and Wales, No. 17517932.

The problem

Mental health support in Arabic is scarce and scattered, often expensive or hard to reach, and there is no trusted way to book, pay and follow up in one place.

What we are building

A clear digital marketplace: the client picks a specialist and a time in their own time zone, pays securely, then follows and rates their sessions. Specialists manage their schedule and fees from a dedicated portal.

How we earn

The client pays the price of a session or package. The specialist receives a fixed fee for each recorded session, and the platform keeps the difference after payment fees and discounts.

What is already built

  • Booking with real availability and automatic time-zone conversion, card payments, and prices in several currencies.
  • A specialist portal (availability, bookings, recording sessions), an admin dashboard, and specialist payout tracking.
  • Post-session ratings and client feedback follow-up, discount codes, and ad campaigns with per-booking source tracking.
  • Pay-per-minute chat and a mobile app: built, and being launched gradually once operations are funded.

Before you decide

The risks, plainly

Business risks

  • The company is at a very early stage and current revenue is symbolic.
  • Demand may not grow as we expect, or the cost of acquiring a client may rise.
  • The quality of the experience depends on a number of specialists.
  • Mental health is a sensitive field for regulation and reputation.

Investment risks

  • You may lose the entire amount.
  • There is no market to sell the stake, and liquidity is very limited.
  • Your percentage may be diluted by new share issues.
  • Profits may be reinvested rather than distributed (under Option 2), and may be affected by taxes and currency changes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the core difference between the two options?

Option 1 is a 5-year share in profits with no ownership and no repayment of the original amount, at a lower price. Option 2 is permanent ownership of registered shares at a higher price per 1%, and your rights continue after year 5.

Can I buy more than 1%?

Yes, up to the 20% on offer in total. If requests exceed what is offered, shares are allocated in the order of signed agreements.

When do I receive profits?

Once a year, after the annual accounts are approved. Under Option 2, distributions depend on a company decision and are not guaranteed.

Who can invest?

This invitation is aimed at experienced or high-net-worth investors who understand the risks of early-stage companies. We ask you to confirm this and to provide proof of identity before signing.

Do I need a lawyer?

We strongly recommend it. We will send you the draft agreement to review with your legal and financial advisers before signing.

How do I pay, and to whom?

By bank transfer to the official account of LAMSA LTD, only after the agreement is signed. We will never ask you to pay anything before that.
